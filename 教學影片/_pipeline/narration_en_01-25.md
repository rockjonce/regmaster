**v3.** RegMaster decides how new registrations are handled once your event fills up, through the 'allow waitlist' setting, which offers three choices. The first lets people keep registering and join a waitlist once you're full, so the next in line is promoted automatically when someone cancels. The second is a waitlist where you, the organizer, pick who gets accepted: paid teams land in a pending-review list and you accept them yourself, ideal for competitions that require vetting.
