開始使用Getting Started

RegMaster 是專業的線上活動報名平台。本手冊寫給主辦方／活動管理者，帶你走完從登入、建立活動到活動結束報到的完整流程。（平台系統管理員功能不在本手冊範圍。）

RegMaster is a professional online event-registration platform. This handbook is for organisers / activity managers and walks through the entire journey from login and event creation to event-day check-in. (Platform system-admin features are out of scope.)

登入後台

Logging in

前往登入頁，輸入帳號與密碼。若已啟用兩步驟驗證（2FA），再輸入驗證器 App 顯示的 6 位數驗證碼。Open the login page and enter your username and password. If two-factor authentication (2FA) is enabled, also enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app.
登入後進入主辦後台。左側為導覽列：上方是「儀表板／所有活動／AI 助理」，下方是「方案與授權／設定」。After login you land in the Organiser Dashboard. The left sidebar holds Dashboard / All Events / AI Assistant at the top and Plans & License / Settings at the bottom.
進入任一活動後，左側會出現「本活動」分組：總覽、設定、表單設計、報名管理、公告、帳務、評分、報到。Inside an event, the sidebar shows a "This Event" group: Hub, Settings, Form Builder, Registrations, Announcements, Payments, Scoring, Check-In.
提示：手機與平板上，左側導覽列會收合為漢堡選單（☰），點擊即可展開。Tip: On phones and tablets the sidebar collapses into a hamburger menu (☰); tap to open it.

📋 基本資訊

📋 Basics

  • 活動名稱、類別、活動形式：用於所有通知與公開頁；形式決定學員/指導者角色。
  • Name, category, format: used across notifications and the public page; format drives student/coach roles.
  • 活動描述：支援基本 HTML，呈現於公開頁「關於這場活動」。可按 🔄 一鍵轉內聯 HTML 把純文字／Markdown 式內容自動轉成排版。
  • Description: supports basic HTML, shown in "About this activity". Click 🔄 To inline HTML to auto-format plain/Markdown text.
  • 時間設定：開放報名時間（可勾「即日起，立即開放」）、報名截止時間（必填）。
  • Timing: registration open time (or tick "Open now"), and a required deadline.
  • 活動日期模式：「單次／單日」（填活動日與起訖時間）或「多梯次／多日」（於下方場次區塊設定）。
  • Date mode: "Single day" (one date + start/end time) or "Multi-session" (configured in the sessions block).

場次／梯次（多梯次模式）

Sessions (multi-session mode)

每個梯次可設名稱、起訖日期時間與名額上限（各梯次獨立計算）。模式可選單選（一人只能選一梯次）或多選。按 ＋ 新增場次 增列，可拖曳排序。

Each session has a name, start/end date-time and a capacity (counted independently). Mode is single-pick or multi-pick. Click ＋ Add session; rows are drag-sortable.

Poster (AI palette) & Brochure PDF (AI autofill)

🏷️ 組別

🏷️ Groups

新增組別並可套用預設組別（國小／國中／高中／大專／社會／教師／公開組…）或自訂。每組可設年齡規則：不限年齡、年齡區間，或出生日期區間。報名時系統會依選手生日自動驗證。團體活動還可開啟要求中文／英文隊名。

Add groups using presets (elementary / junior / senior high / college / adult / teacher / open…) or custom names. Each group can have an age rule: none, age range, or birth-date range — validated against the registrant's birthday automatically. Team events can also require a Chinese / English team name.

💳 收費

💳 Fees

  • 收費項目：可新增多個項目（報名費／材料費／保證金…），系統自動加總顯示「收費總額」。
  • Fee items: add multiple items (registration / materials / deposit…); the total is summed automatically.
  • 啟用線上付款（PayUNI）：開關開啟後報名者可線上刷卡，付款成功自動對帳。
  • Enable PayUNI: lets registrants pay by card online; payments auto-reconcile on success.
  • 銀行 / ATM 轉帳：開啟「啟用銀行轉帳收款與對帳」後，填寫銀行（下拉）→ 分行代號（下拉自動帶出）→ 帳號 → 戶名。此帳戶會顯示給報名者直接匯款，且「帳務」頁的快速銀行對帳才會啟用。
  • Bank / ATM transfer: enable it, then fill Bank (dropdown) → Branch (auto-filled dropdown) → Account → Holder. This account is shown to registrants for direct transfer, and it unlocks Quick Bank Reconciliation on the Payments page.
  • 折扣碼：建立折抵金額或百分比的代碼，可設使用上限與有效期限，供報名者結帳時輸入。
  • Discount codes: create fixed-amount or percentage codes with usage limits and expiry for registrants to enter at checkout.
金流分流：PayUNI 由平台代收（扣手續費後依週期匯給您）；銀行/ATM 轉帳由報名者直接匯入您填的帳戶，平台不經手。Money split: PayUNI is collected by the platform (paid to you on a cycle after fees); bank/ATM transfers go directly into your account and never touch the platform.

📢 通知

📢 Notifications

可開關自動 Email 通知（關閉後新報名者不收確認信）。此分頁也內含活動公告編輯器：填日期、標題、內容後按 ＋ 新增公告，公告會即時顯示在公開頁「公告」區（由新到舊）。需要群發 EDM 請見「公告與 EDM」章節。

Toggle automatic email notifications (off = new registrants get no confirmation email). This tab also has an inline announcement editor: fill date/title/content and click ＋ Add announcement; it appears instantly on the public page (newest first). For mass EDM see the Announcements chapter.

⚙️ 進階

⚙️ Advanced

  • 報名上限（0 = 無上限）與允許候補（是＝額滿後寫「備取」；否＝額滿即停收）。多梯次模式下，上限為各梯次加總、唯讀。
  • Capacity (0 = unlimited) and Waitlist (Yes = overflow becomes "waitlist"; No = stop when full). In multi-session mode this is the read-only sum of per-session caps.
  • 可見性：「於公開瀏覽頁顯示」與「開放報名」兩個開關。
  • Visibility: "Show on public listing" and "Open for registration" toggles.
  • 危險區域：複製活動（複製設定但不含報名）與 刪除活動（需輸入活動名稱二次確認，永久且不可復原）。
  • Danger zone: Duplicate (copies settings, not registrations) and Delete (type the event name to confirm; permanent).

常見問題FAQ

快速銀行對帳要輸入幾碼？

How many digits for quick bank reconciliation?

輸入銀行帳號末 5 碼，與報名者填寫的「轉出帳號後 5 碼」比對，自動標記相符且未確認的隊伍為「已確認」。

Enter the account's last 5 digits; it matches the "account last 5 digits" registrants provide and marks matching unconfirmed teams as confirmed.

平台多久匯款一次？銀行轉帳也會被平台代收嗎？

How often does the platform pay out? Are bank transfers collected too?

平台依轉帳週期匯款：每週（每週四）或每月（每月 5 日，遇假日順延）。只有 PayUNI 線上付款由平台代收並扣手續費後匯給您；銀行/ATM 轉帳由報名者直接匯入您帳戶，不計入平台代收轉付。下次匯款日可在「帳務 → 平台代收轉付」查看。

The platform pays out on a cycle: weekly (Thursdays) or monthly (5th, rolling forward on weekends). Only PayUNI is collected by the platform (paid to you after fees); bank/ATM transfers go directly into your account and aren't part of payouts. See the next date under Payments → Platform payouts.

報名上限存檔後數字會不見嗎？

Does the capacity reset after saving?

不會，會正確保留。多梯次模式下，總上限是各梯次名額的加總（唯讀），需到「場次/梯次」調整。

No, it persists. In multi-session mode the total is the read-only sum of per-session caps — adjust them in the Sessions block.

為什麼一張卡片只能填一位？

Why one person per block?

RegMaster 採「1 區塊 = 1 人」原則，讓人數計算與資料驗證更精準。要收多位就在表單設計器新增多個學員/指導者區塊。附加問題區塊則固定只有一個。

RegMaster uses "1 block = 1 person" for accurate counting and validation. Add more student/coach blocks in the Form Builder for more people. The additional-questions block is always single.

收款帳戶和活動收費的銀行帳戶有什麼不同？

Payout account vs. event bank account?

收款帳戶（設定頁）= 平台把 PayUNI 實領匯給您的帳戶。活動收費的銀行帳戶（活動設定→收費）= 報名者 ATM 直接匯入的帳戶。兩者用途不同，請分別填寫。

The payout account (Settings) is where the platform wires your PayUNI net. The event bank account (Settings→Fees) is where registrants transfer directly. They serve different purposes — fill both as needed.
