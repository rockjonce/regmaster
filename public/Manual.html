認識 RegMasterAbout RegMaster

RegMaster 是專為認真辦活動的人打造的線上報名系統。本手冊寫給主辦方與其團隊成員，帶你走完從登入、建立活動、設計表單、收費，到報名管理、錄取、報到與評分的完整流程。

RegMaster is an online registration platform built for people who run events seriously. This manual is for organisers and their team members, walking you through the full journey — sign in, create an event, design the form, collect fees, then manage registrations, accept teams, check in, and score.

RegMaster 能幫你做什麼

What RegMaster does for you

一站式活動後台One console for everything

建立活動、拖拉式表單設計、收費與線上付款、報名與帳務、報到、評分、證書 — 全部在同一個後台完成。Create events, drag-and-drop forms, payments, registrations, billing, check-in, scoring and certificates — all in one place.

個人或團體報名Individual or team registration

支援個人報名、團體報名與含指導者的隊伍；表單以「1 區塊＝1 人」原則自由組合。Individual, team, or team-with-coach formats; the form is composed with a simple "1 block = 1 person" rule.

收費、折扣與退費Fees, discounts & refunds

PayUNI 線上刷卡或銀行轉帳、折扣碼、依法規範本的退費政策、平台代收轉付。PayUNI online payments or bank transfer, discount codes, statutory refund policies, and platform-collected payouts.

自訂正取、報到與評分Custom acceptance, check-in & scoring

自行決定正取名單、QR 自助報到、多評審公平評分管線、證書與名牌批次產出。Pick who gets accepted, QR self check-in, a fair multi-judge scoring pipeline, and batch certificate/badge generation.

名詞速記：「活動」＝你舉辦的一場賽事/研習/營隊；「報名」＝一筆報名資料（個人或一隊）；「正取／備取／審核中」＝報名的錄取狀態。Glossary: "Event" = one competition/workshop/camp you run; "Registration" = one entry (a person or a team); "Accepted / Waitlisted / Pending review" = the entry's acceptance status.

登入與主辦後台Sign In & Console

用主辦方帳號登入後即進入主辦後台。後台左側為固定導覽列，分「概覽」與「管理」兩區。

Sign in with your organiser account to enter the console. A fixed left navigation is split into "Overview" and "Manage".

登入方式

Ways to sign in

  • 帳號密碼：在登入頁輸入帳號與密碼，可勾「30 天內保持登入」。Username & password: on the login page; tick "Keep me signed in for 30 days" if you like.
  • Google／LINE：若你的帳號已綁定社群登入，可一鍵登入。Google / LINE: one-click sign-in if your account is linked.
  • 登出：左側欄底部的「登出」。切換帳號時會回到登入頁。Sign out: at the bottom of the left nav. Switching accounts returns to the login page.
忘記密碼可於登入頁點「忘記密碼？」以 Email 重設。報名者（隊伍）有各自的查詢密碼，與主辦方帳號不同。Use "Forgot password?" on the login page to reset by email. Registrants (teams) have their own lookup password, separate from your organiser account.

建立活動Create an Event

在「所有活動」頁點右上角的建立活動，填三個基本欄位即建立。

On the "All events" page, click Create event at the top right and fill three basics.

活動名稱：對外顯示的名稱（之後仍可改）。Event name: shown publicly (editable later).
類別：如競賽、研習、營隊等，用於分類與篩選。Category: e.g. competition, workshop, camp — used for filtering.
活動形式：個人報名、團體報名、或團體含指導者。這決定表單預設的人員區塊。Format: individual, team, or team-with-coach — this seeds the form's person blocks.
按建立 →（或在名稱欄按 Enter）即建立，並直接進入該活動的總覽。Click Create → (or press Enter in the name field) to create and jump straight to the Hub.
新活動預設為草稿（未公開、未開放報名），你可以慢慢設定，設定完成再開放。A new event starts as a draft (not public, not open). Configure it at your own pace, then open it.

Every event has a Hub — the entry point to all its tools. The "This event" nav lists Settings, Form Builder, Registrations, Billing, Scoring, Check-in, Certificates and more.

基本設定與時間Basics & Dates

活動設定頁分多個分頁。「基本」分頁處理名稱、類別、活動說明、開放報名時間、截止時間與活動日期（含多梯次）。

The Settings page has several tabs. "Basics" covers name, category, description, open time, deadline, and the event date(s) — including multiple sessions.

活動說明與 AI 摘要

Description & AI summary

活動說明支援多段文字。儲存時，系統可用 AI 產生一段適合公開頁 hero 的精簡摘要 — 這是草稿，需你按「核准發布」後才會公開。The description supports rich text. On save, the system can draft a concise summary for the public hero — it's a draft and only goes live after you click "Approve & publish".

時間：開放、截止、活動日

Times: open, deadline, event date

  • 開放報名時間：可指定未來某時刻自動開放，或勾「即日起」立即開放。Open time: schedule a future moment, or tick "Open now" for immediate opening.
  • 報名截止：到時間後公開頁顯示「已截止」。Deadline: after this the public page shows "Closed".
  • 活動日：單日活動填一個日期；多梯次活動改用「梯次」清單。Event date: a single date for one-day events; use the "Sessions" list for multi-session events.

多梯次（場次）

Multiple sessions

切到多梯次模式後，可新增多個梯次，各自有名稱、起訖日期時間與該梯名額。報名者在第 1 步選擇要報名哪個梯次。系統會擋下「結束早於開始」的梯次。Switch to multi-session mode to add sessions, each with a name, start/end datetime, and its own capacity. Registrants pick a session in step 1. Sessions where "end is before start" are rejected on save.

退費政策的活動日：多梯次活動沒有單一活動日，退費級距會以最早梯次的起始日計算距活動天數。Refund date for multi-session: with no single event date, refund tiers count days-to-event from the earliest session start.

組別、年齡與隊名Groups, Age & Team Name

在「組別」分頁設定報名分組、各組年齡規則，以及是否要求隊名。

The "Groups" tab sets registration groups, per-group age rules, and whether a team name is required.

新增組別

Add groups

從預設組別下拉選一個（如國中組、高中組）後按「＋新增組別」，或選「自訂」建立自訂名稱。報名者在第 1 步從你建立的組別中選擇。Pick a preset (e.g. Junior, Senior) and click "+ Add group", or choose "Custom" for a custom name. Registrants pick from your groups in step 1.

年齡規則（報名時自動驗證）

Age rules (auto-checked at registration)

每個組別可設年齡上下限（或允許出生日期範圍）。報名時系統會用學員生日自動驗證，不符會被擋下並提示。Each group can set min/max age (or an allowed birth-date range). At registration the system auto-validates against each student's birthday and blocks mismatches with a clear message.

重要：年齡自動驗證需要表單裡有「生日」欄位。若你設了年齡規則卻沒有生日欄位，儲存時會出現提醒 — 請到表單設計加入生日欄位。Important: auto age-check needs a Birthday field in the form. If you set age rules without one, you'll get a reminder on save — add a Birthday field in the Form Builder.

是否要求隊名

Require a team name?

開啟「要求中文隊名」後，報名第 1 步會出現必填的隊名欄；關閉則整個隊名區塊隱藏。沒有隊名的個人報名，後台會以報名者姓名辨識。Turn on "Require a Chinese team name" to show a required team-name field in step 1; turning it off hides the whole block. For nameless individual entries, the console identifies them by the registrant's name.

拖拉式表單設計器，原則是「1 區塊＝1 人」：每個學員、每位指導者各一個區塊。左側欄位面板拖到中間畫布，右側檢視器設定該欄位。

A drag-and-drop builder with one rule: "1 block = 1 person" — one block per student, one per coach. Drag fields from the left palette onto the canvas, then configure them in the right inspector.

內建欄位 vs 自訂欄位

Built-in vs custom fields

面板裡的內建特殊欄位（中文姓名、Email、生日、身分證、電話…）帶有對應的驗證與系統用途；你也可以新增自訂欄位（文字、下拉、單複選、日期…）。建議 Email 與生日盡量用面板裡的內建欄位，系統才能正確寄信與驗證年齡。Built-in special fields in the palette (name, Email, birthday, ID, phone…) carry their own validation and system purpose. You can also add custom fields (text, dropdown, choices, date…). Prefer the built-in Email and Birthday so the system can email and age-check correctly.

開放報名的前置條件

Prerequisite to open registration

表單至少要有一個必填 Email 欄位（學員或指導者皆可），否則無法開放報名（無法寄確認信）。另外，必填的下拉/單選若沒有任何選項會擋下儲存，避免報名者卡關。The form must have at least one required Email field (student or coach) before registration can open (otherwise no confirmation email). Also, a required dropdown/radio with zero options is rejected on save, so registrants never get stuck.

收費、折扣與退費Fees, Discounts & Refunds

「收費」分頁設定報名費項目、折扣碼、退費政策與付款方式。

The "Fees" tab sets fee items, discount codes, refund policy, and payment methods.

費用項目

Fee items

可加多個收費項目，系統自動加總為報名費。費用不可為負數（後端會擋）。Add multiple fee items; the total becomes the registration fee. Negative amounts are rejected by the server.

付款方式

Payment methods

  • PayUNI 線上付款：報名者線上刷卡，平台代收，扣手續費後依週期匯款給你。PayUNI online: card payment collected by the platform; paid out to you on a cycle after fees.
  • 銀行／ATM 轉帳：填寫你的收款銀行、帳號與戶名；報名者直接匯入你的帳戶，不經平台。Bank / ATM transfer: enter your bank, account and holder name; registrants transfer directly to you, bypassing the platform.
啟用線上付款與銀行轉帳為二擇一的互鎖設計；啟用銀行轉帳時，銀行欄位需完整填寫才能儲存。Online payment and bank transfer are mutually exclusive; enabling bank transfer requires complete bank details to save.

折扣碼

Discount codes

可建立折扣碼（定額或比例），設定使用上限。報名者在付款頁輸入後，金額即時重算。折扣碼在「套用當下」即消耗一次使用次數（每隊每碼僅扣一次）。Create codes (fixed or percentage) with a usage cap. Registrants enter a code on the payment page and the total recalculates instantly. A code consumes one use the moment it's applied (once per team per code).

退費政策

Refund policy

從法規範本（旅遊型、課程型）或平台建議範本選一個，系統自動帶入「距活動天數 → 退費比例」級距，可再優待但不得低於法定下限。報名者線上申請退費時依此試算。Pick a statutory template (travel, course) or the platform's suggestion; the system fills the "days-to-event → refund %" tiers. You may be more generous but not below the legal floor. Registrant refund requests are quoted from this.

退費基準＝折後實付金額。套用折扣的報名，退費以實付金額乘上退費比例計算，不會以原價多退。選「不啟用」即關閉線上退費（報名者改聯繫主辦方）。Refunds are based on the amount actually paid after discounts — never the list price. Choose "Disabled" to turn off online refunds (registrants contact you instead).

名額與候補／正取模式Capacity & Acceptance Mode

「進階」分頁設定報名上限與「允許候補」模式 — 這決定額滿後怎麼處理、以及正取名單怎麼產生。

The "Advanced" tab sets the capacity and the acceptance mode — how overflow is handled and how the accepted list is formed.

報名上限

Capacity

報名上限一律採 -1 = 無上限、0 = 不開放、N = 上限（有無組別皆同）。沒有組別時，直接在這裡（或各梯次）填；多梯次的總上限是各梯次的加總。有組別時，容量改由「組別上限」層級決定，此欄與各梯次上限一律自動加總並唯讀 — 詳見下一節〈報名上限（組別 × 梯次 層級）〉。Caps use -1 = unlimited, 0 = closed, N = a cap everywhere (with or without groups). Without groups, enter it here (or per session); the multi-session total is the sum. With groups, capacity is driven by per-group caps and this field (and each session cap) becomes an auto-summed, read-only total — see the next section, "Registration Caps (Group × Session)".

報名上限（組別 × 梯次 層級）Registration Caps (Group × Session)

報名上限採「組別 → 梯次 → 總數」由下往上加總的層級。你只需要設定最底層的「組別上限」，梯次上限與總報名上限都會自動算出並唯讀，避免兩套數字打架。

Capacity is a bottom-up hierarchy: Group → Session → Total. You only set the bottom layer — each group's cap — and the session and total caps are computed automatically (read-only), so the numbers never conflict.

三種數值的意思

What the numbers mean

數值Value意思Meaning
-1無上限 — 該組不限人數。Unlimited — no limit for this group.
0不開放 — 該組（在該梯次）不收報名。Closed — this group is not open (for that session).
N上限 N 人（正整數）。Cap of N (a positive integer).
-1（無上限）僅在你的方案已解鎖大型活動容量時可用；未解鎖請填具體數字或升級方案。這套 -1/0/N 語意對「有組別」與「無組別（全域／各梯次）」一致適用。-1 (unlimited) is only allowed when your plan has unlocked large-event capacity; otherwise enter a number or upgrade. The -1/0/N convention applies uniformly to grouped and non-grouped (global / per-session) caps.

評分Scoring

建立多層評分量表、分派評審，並以公平管線得到排名。多評審評分為 Pro／Team 方案功能。

Build a multi-level rubric, assign judges, and rank with a fair pipeline. Multi-judge scoring is a Pro/Team feature.

評分量表

Rubric

建立最多三層樹狀量表（主項目 → 細項 → 子細項）。每層權重需加總 100%（系統即時驗證）；葉項目設滿分、可記錄次數、多次取值方式與正規化方式（預設 t 分布）。Build up to three levels (main → sub → leaf). Each level's weights must sum to 100% (validated live). Leaf items set max score, record count, aggregation, and normalisation (default t-distribution).

評審組群與分派

Panels & assignment

建立評審組群，指定報名組別、該組評審與負責隊伍。只有管理者與評審角色可被指派為評審。若你（擁有者或管理者）也被指派，評分頁會出現🎯 以評審身分評分入口。Create panels with a group, its judges, and assigned teams. Only manager and judge roles can be assigned. If you (owner/manager) are assigned, a 🎯 Score as judge entry appears.

公平性開關

Fairness

  • 評審可看到即時排名：預設關閉，避免分數互相錨定。Judges see live ranking: off by default to avoid anchoring.
  • 評審可看到報名者個資：預設關閉，關閉時評審只看到代稱。Judges see registrant info: off by default; judges see aliases only.
  • 合併方式、去極值（去最高最低）可設定；備取與審核中隊伍不列入評分名單。Aggregation and trim-extremes are configurable; waitlisted/pending teams are excluded from scoring.

結果與排名口徑

Results & ranking

總排名以「原始加權分」排序（跨組可比），分數欄也顯示原始加權分；分組檢視則顯示組內正規化分。未評分／備取不列入排名。隊伍少於 5 支時，進階正規化自動退回線性並顯示小樣本提示。可匯出 Excel／CSV。Overall ranks use the raw weighted score (comparable across groups), and the score column shows it too; the in-group view shows normalised scores. Unscored/waitlisted teams aren't ranked. With fewer than 5 teams, advanced normalisation falls back to linear with a small-sample note. Export to Excel/CSV.

常見問題FAQ

主辦方最常問的幾個問題。更多操作性疑難可用上方「問題快速定位」。

The questions organisers ask most. For operational issues, use "Quick Problem Finder" above.

Q. 報名上限存檔後數字會不見嗎？

Q. Does the capacity number disappear after saving?

不會。報名上限會被正確保存。若你用「組別上限」，重新載入仍會保留你設的數字；多梯次模式下，總上限是各梯次名額的加總。詳見〈報名上限（組別 × 梯次 層級）〉。No. Capacity is saved correctly. Group caps persist across reloads; in multi-session mode the total is the sum of session caps. See "Registration Caps (Group × Session)".

Q. 有組別時，怎麼設「真正的」報名上限？為什麼梯次上限改不動？

Q. With groups, how do I set the real cap? Why can't I edit the session cap?

有組別時，容量以「組別上限」為唯一來源：到「組別」分頁每組卡片右側填「報名上限」。梯次上限與總數會自動＝加總並唯讀（避免兩套數字打架）。要改總量就回「組別」改組別上限；要讓不同梯次不同就用進階矩陣（見下一題）。With groups, group caps are the single source: set each group's "Cap" on the Groups tab. Session and total caps become an auto-summed, read-only value. To change the total, edit the group caps; for per-session differences, use the advanced matrix (next question).

Q. 組別上限填的 -1、0 是什麼意思？

Q. What do -1 and 0 mean in a group cap?

-1 = 無上限（不限人數，需方案已解鎖）、0 = 不開放（不收報名）、N = 上限 N 人。這套語意對「有組別（組別上限）」與「無組別（全域／各梯次上限）」一致適用。-1 = unlimited (needs an unlocked plan), 0 = closed (not open), N = a cap of N. This applies uniformly to grouped (group caps) and non-grouped (global / per-session) caps.

Q. 想讓不同梯次收不同人數（例：南區某組少收）怎麼設？

Q. How do I set different caps for different sessions?

勾選「梯次」清單下方的「各梯次設定不同組別上限（進階）」，展開 (梯次 × 組別) 矩陣後逐格填數字。開啟後「組別」分頁的組別上限會變唯讀、改以矩陣為準。Tick "Set different group caps per session (advanced)" below the Sessions list to open a (session × group) matrix and fill each cell. While it's on, the Groups-tab caps become read-only and the matrix takes over.

Q.「自訂正取」和「依報名順序正取」差在哪？

Q. Custom acceptance vs. by-order?

依報名順序＝額滿前先到先得、額滿後自動候補；自訂正取＝全部付款後進「審核中」，由你逐一勾選正取（勾選即扣名額、寄錄取信、單向鎖不可改回）。詳見〈名額與候補／正取模式〉與〈自訂正取〉。By-order = first-come-first-served then auto-waitlist; custom = everyone pays into "Pending review", then you accept one by one (uses a slot, sends an email, one-way lock). See the Acceptance and Custom Acceptance sections.

Q. 套了折扣碼，退費以原價還是折後計？

Q. After a discount, are refunds on full price or paid amount?

以折後實付金額為基準，不會以原價多退。Always the amount actually paid after discounts — never the list price.

Q. 銀行轉帳的款項會被平台代收嗎？

Q. Are bank transfers collected by the platform?

不會。銀行／ATM 轉帳由報名者直接匯入你的帳戶，不經平台、不計入代收轉付。只有 PayUNI 線上付款由平台代收。No. Bank/ATM transfers go straight to your account, bypassing the platform. Only PayUNI online payments are platform-collected.

Q. 未獲正取的人怎麼退費？

Q. How do I refund people who weren't accepted?

在帳務頁點「備取／未正取 一鍵退款」，可一次全額退款所有已付款但未錄取者，並寄出附「未獲錄取」說明的退費通知。In Billing, click "Refund all waitlisted / pending" to fully refund every paid-but-not-accepted entry and send a notice with a "not accepted" note.

Q. 評審看得到別隊的分數或排名嗎？

Q. Can judges see other teams' scores or ranking?

預設看不到。「評審可看到即時排名」與「評審可看到報名者個資」皆預設關閉，避免分數互相錨定。No by default. "Judges see live ranking" and "Judges see registrant info" are both off by default to avoid anchoring.

Q. 總排名的分數和名次看起來對不上？

Q. The score and rank seem inconsistent in the overall list?

總排名以「原始加權分」排序（跨組可比），分數欄顯示的也是原始加權分；分組檢視則顯示組內正規化分。未評分與備取／審核中不列入排名。Overall ranks use the raw weighted score (comparable across groups), shown in the score column too; the in-group view shows normalised scores. Unscored and waitlisted/pending teams aren't ranked.

Q. 證書可以發給指導老師（感謝狀）嗎？

Q. Can certificates go to coaches (thank-you cards)?

可以。匯出時對象範圍選「僅指導者」，文字用稱謂 token 帶出職稱，即可批次產出感謝狀。Yes. Set the export scope to "coaches only" and use a salutation token to batch-produce thank-you certificates.
